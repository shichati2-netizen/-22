כתב-יד AI — קרדיטים ורישיונות של נתונים ומקורות
KtavYad AI — data and source credits

מודל הזיהוי אומן על כתבי יד שתרמו משתמשי התוכנה, ועל המאגרים הציבוריים הבאים.
עם התוכנה מופץ רק המודל המאומן. התמונות והתמלולים של המאגרים לא מופצים.
The recognition model was trained on handwriting donated by the application's users and on the public datasets below.
Only the trained model is distributed; no images or transcriptions from these datasets are included.

1. Pinkas dataset — I. Rabaev, B. Kurar Barakat, J. El-Sana, "The Pinkas Dataset", Zenodo 2019,
   doi:10.5281/zenodo.3569694. Licence: CC BY 4.0. Line crops via cyttic/pinkas-hebrew-lines (CC BY 4.0).

2. HTR Winter School 2025 (University of Vienna), Hebrew group: transcription of BL Or. 10037 (Mishneh Torah),
   github.com/HTR-School-Vienna/2025-hebrew. Licence: CC BY 4.0 / CC BY-SA. Images: The British Library.

3. Sofer Mahir — D. Stökl Ben Ezra and H. Lapin, Sofer Mahir database, Paris 2022 (github.com/dstoekl/sofer_mahir),
   transcription of Munich, Bayerische Staatsbibliothek, Cod. hebr. 117. Licence of the transcriptions: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
   (permission for this use has been requested from the authors). Images: BSB / MDZ, Public Domain Mark.

4. HHD — I. Rabaev, B. Kurar Barakat, A. Churkin, J. El-Sana, "The HHD Dataset", ICFHR 2020,
   doi:10.1109/ICFHR2020.2020.00050 (HHD_age, Zenodo 14996257; HHD_gender, Zenodo 4729908), via the line-level copies
   cyttic/trocr-hebrew-matan and cyttic/trocr-hebrew-human (permission for this use has been requested from the authors).

5. DiffusionPen synthetic Hebrew handwriting (cyttic/diffusionpen-hebrew-handwriting, CC BY 4.0) — rehearsal lines.

6. Language model: texts from Sefaria (sefaria.org; public-domain and CC-licensed texts as listed by Sefaria).

7. Fonts of the interface (bundled in gui/fonts, used only for display): Assistant — Copyright 2010 The Assistant
   Project Authors (github.com/hafontia/Assistant); Frank Ruhl Libre — Copyright 2015 The Frank Ruhl Libre Project
   Authors (github.com/fontef/frankruhllibre). Both under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (scripts.sil.org/OFL); the
   licence texts are included next to the font files.

תודה רבה לכל החוקרים והמוסדות שפרסמו את הנתונים האלה.
